Academic Support

Study Matrials - We provides a rigorous and inclusive space for undergraduates to collaborate, learn, and grow, driven by peer pedagogy. These study materials serves more than 1,000 undergraduates annually and is experienced in supporting all students.

Past Physics Notes - PhySoc has worked with physics students at IIT KGP to collect the notes of students from previous physics courses they enrolled in. These notes are attached and separated by course; they may prove an excellent resource for studying or surveying the content of a course you plan to take!

Academic Wing

PAW -This year PhySoc has launched the PhySoc Academic Wing (PAW) – a new initiative for second year students and above. PAW promotes peer-to-peer learning by encouraging senior-led discussions on specialized research topics between seniors and juniors. In addition to these research deep dives, we also host regular lectures on fundamental physics concepts, connecting core coursework to cutting edge science.

Academic Wing Meeting -We host PhySoc-AWM, weekly research-mentorship sessions on Saturdays from 3:00PM to 5:00PM. Students get a chance to discuss and explore niche domains such as gravitational physics, biophysics, plasma physics and geophysics, mentored by senior and graduate students. This program connects coursework and research and provides junior students with the foundation and vision to pursue advanced projects and academic careers.
