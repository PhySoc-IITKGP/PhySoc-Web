Our physics advisors are here for you, so don’t hesitate to reach out.

Academic Wing

PAW - PhySoc Lanched Academic Wing this year. PAW supports all students who are deeply interested in physics, but the coursework is not able to satisfy them enough.

Academic Wing Meeting - We host AWMs, weekly research-mentorship sessions on Saturdays from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM. Senior and graduate mentors guide discussions to help students explore niche domains such as gravitational physics, biophysics, plasma physics and geophysics. This initiative links coursework and research and gives junior students the basis and vision to continue in advanced projects and academic careers.

Academic Support

Study Matrials - We provides a rigorous and inclusive space for undergraduates to collaborate, learn, and grow, driven by peer pedagogy. These study materials serves more than 1,000 undergraduates annually and is experienced in supporting all students.

Past Physics Notes - PhySoc has worked with physics students at IIT KGP to collect the notes of students from previous physics courses they enrolled in. These notes are attached and separated by course; they may prove an excellent resource for studying or surveying the content of a course you plan to take!
