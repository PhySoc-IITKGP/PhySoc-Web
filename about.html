About Us

The Physics Society
of IIT Kharagpur

Founded on the belief that curiosity is the cornerstone of discovery, PhySoc has been the heartbeat of physics culture at IIT KGP since its inception. We are students, researchers, and dreamers — united by a love for the universe.

Our Mission

To cultivate a vibrant intellectual community where every student can explore physics beyond the classroom — through lectures, research, collaboration, and hands-on experimentation.

  • Host weekly talks, seminars, and reading groups on frontier physics
  • Facilitate student-faculty research connections (UROP)
  • Maintain shared study and resource repositories
  • Represent physics students on academic governance bodies

Our Values

What We Do

Lectures Series

Weekly student-led talks on topics from condensed matter to cosmology. Interesed student may mail us for presentataion.

UROP Projects

We connect undergraduates with department research groups for semester and summer projects.

Physics Sandbox

Interactive simulations of orbital mechanics, double pendulum chaos, and more built by our members.

Resource Library

Curated textbooks, lecture notes, and problem sets for every semester of the BSc/BTech Physics curriculum.

Weekly Puzzles

Every week a new physics problem is posted — from olympiad-style to research-level curiosities.

Monthly Departmental Lab Visit

Monthly guided tours and live demonstrations at the department's research facilities.
