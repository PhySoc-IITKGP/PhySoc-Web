IIT Kharagpur

Department of Physics

Established in 1951, the Department of Physics at IIT Kharagpur has a distinguished history of pioneering research and academic excellence. From fundamental theoretical quantum mechanics to advanced nanodevices and cosmology.

About the Department

The Department hosts over 40 distinguished faculty members, 150+ research scholars, and state-of-the-art national research facilities. Departmental talks and seminars take place in the historic H.N. Bose Seminar Room.

Main Department Location: Physics Building, IIT Kharagpur, WB 721302

Department Research Laboratories

Advanced Condensed Matter (FIST)

Cryogenic transport, high magnetic field measurements, and thin film heterostructures.

Energy Materials & Storage Lab

3D printing of energy devices, sodium-ion batteries, and supercapacitors.

Quantum Optics & Laser Physics

Ultrafast spectroscopy, microresonators, nonlinear photonics, and optical fiber sensors.

HPC Supercomputing & Astrophysics

Parallel HPC clusters for 21-cm cosmology, AdS/CFT simulations, and DFT modelling.

Nuclear Physics & Radiation Lab

Gamma-ray spectroscopy, double beta decay simulations, and nuclear detector systems.

Spintronics & Microscience Lab

Magnetic skyrmions, spin vortices, and quantum transport in 2D heterostructures.
