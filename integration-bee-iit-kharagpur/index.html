INTEGRATION BEE

What is Integration Bee?

The Integration Bee is PhySoc IIT Kharagpur’s premier annual calculus tournament, inspired by the renowned collegiate math bees. It brings together undergraduate and postgraduate students from all departments to put their integration prowess, analytical agility, and creative problem-solving techniques to the test against tricky, non-trivial integrals under strict time constraints.

Whether you are a fresher mastering standard substitutions or an advanced enthusiast deploying Feynman’s trick, complex contour integration, and clever algebraic symmetries, the Integration Bee provides an exhilarating, high-energy arena for celebrating the sheer elegance of mathematics.

Ready to take on the challenge? register here.

Competition Format

Qualifier (9:00 a.m. – 12:00 p.m.)

  • Written test: 20 integrals in 60 minutes
  • Top 16 scorers qualify for playoffs

Playoffs (1:00 p.m. – 4:00 p.m.)

  • Single-elimination tournament on the blackboard
  • Top 16 participate, remaining contestants cheer them in the audience.

Round 1: 16 → 8 (first to solve 1 integral, 2 minutes)
Round 2: 8 → 4 (first to solve 1 integral, 2 minutes)
Round 3: 4 → 2 (first to solve 1 integral, 3 minutes )
Final: 2 → Champion (first to solve 2 integrals, 3 minutes each)
