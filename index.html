About us

Welcome to PhySoc, the official student-led Physics Society of the Department of Physics at IIT Kharagpur. We are a passionate collective of undergraduates, postgraduates, and physics enthusiasts dedicated to cultivating scientific curiosity and community across campus.

Our society serves as a bridge between faculty and students, organizing flagship seminar series, research colloquia, interactive guidance sessions, and academic competitions. We provide mentorship for internships, research pathways, and postgraduate opportunities while encouraging interdisciplinary exploration.

Whether you are exploring theoretical physics, condensed matter, quantum computing, astrophysics, or simply have a love for scientific discovery, PhySoc provides a vibrant platform to learn, collaborate, and grow.
