Who We Are

The Physics Community at IIT Kharagpur

PhySoc is the student-led physics society of IIT Kharagpur — bringing together passionate minds to explore, discuss, and celebrate the science that governs our universe. From quantum mechanics to astrophysics, from Fundae Talks to inter-IIT competitions, we are your community for everything physics.

Academic Excellence
Revision sessions, past papers, study groups and exam guidance — we've got your back.
Research Culture
Connecting students to research labs, TIFR, IISc, international internships, and PhD pathways.
Vibrant Community
Events, socials, competitions, lab tours — a home for everyone curious about physics at KGP.
