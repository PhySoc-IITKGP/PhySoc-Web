Welcome to PhySoc! Physics Society IITKGP

As members of the PhySoc, we work within the department to provide a supportive community for everyone interested in physics:

  • Hosting esteemed researchers to share recent breakthroughs and developments in modern physics.
  • Providing a platform for seniors to share their research experiences, publications, and methodologies.
  • Conducting "Fundae Talks" on internships, research fellowships, and PhD pathways to guide incoming batches.
  • Encouraging student competence, research enthusiasm, and scientific responsibility within the department.
  • Building collaborative bridges between IIT Kharagpur students and physics societies across other premier institutes.
