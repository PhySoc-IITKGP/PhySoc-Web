⚛️ De Broglie Atomic Standing Wave

Visualizing Bohr's quantization condition mvr = nℏ (2πr = nλ). Demonstrates constructive standing electron waves along circular orbits vs non-integer destructive collapse.

General Relativity simulation solving 2nd-order geodesic differential equations around a black hole with RK4 integration. Displays Event Horizon (2M), Photon Sphere (3M), ISCO (6M), and perihelion precession.
