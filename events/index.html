Funde Talk

Funde Talks are informal mentorship and guidance sessions hosted by senior students and alumni. These interactive catch-ups help students navigate academics, research internships, department electives, career pathways, and life at IIT Kharagpur in a relaxed, friendly setting.

Physics Talk

Physics Talks are our flagship seminar-style events where professors, distinguished researchers, and guest speakers present fascinating topics from quantum physics, astrophysics, condensed matter, and emerging frontiers. A great space to learn, explore, and ask questions.

Competitions

PhySoc organizes challenging and fun physics competitions, quizzes, and problem-solving contests throughout the year. Test your conceptual understanding, collaborate with peers, and win exciting prizes in department-wide academic events.

Lab Tours

Lab Tours offer students an insider's look into state-of-the-art physics research laboratories across the department. Guided by faculty and research scholars, participants explore experimental setups, advanced equipment, and ongoing cutting-edge scientific research.
