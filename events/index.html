Events & Activities

BEAN TIME

Bean Time is PhySoc's regular casual coffee catch-up for physics students and enthusiasts. Drop by to grab a coffee (or any hot drink), take a break from classes, and chat with fellow students about physics, uni life, or anything else on your mind. It's a relaxed way to meet people in the community and unwind between lectures or study sessions.

PHYSICS TALKS

Physics Talks are PhySoc's seminar-style events where students, researchers, and guest speakers share ideas from across physics and related fields. These talks discuss current research, emerging technologies, and fascinating concepts in an accessible way. They're a great opportunity to learn something new, ask questions, and engage with the physics community.

LAB TOURS

Lab Tours give students the chance to visit physics research laboratories and see how real experiments are conducted. Guided by researchers or lab members, you'll learn about the equipment, techniques, and projects being developed at the university. It's a great way to explore different areas of physics research and see what working in a lab is really like.
