Funde Talk

Funde Talks are PhySoc's signature informal guidance sessions — a relaxed catch-up between seniors and juniors. Drop by to discuss academics, elective choices, research internships, career pathways, and life at IIT Kharagpur. It's a warm, open space to meet people, share experiences, and unwind between lectures or study sessions.

Physics Talks

Physics Talks are PhySoc's seminar-style events where students, researchers, and guest speakers share ideas from across physics and related fields. These talks discuss current research, emerging technologies, and fascinating concepts in an accessible way. They're a great opportunity to learn something new, ask questions, and engage with the physics community.

Lab Tours

Lab Tours offer students an insider's look into state-of-the-art physics research laboratories across the department. Guided by faculty and research scholars, participants explore experimental setups, advanced equipment, and cutting-edge scientific research happening right here on campus. A rare chance to see real physics in action.

Competitions

PhySoc organizes challenging and fun physics competitions, quizzes, and problem-solving contests throughout the year. Test your conceptual understanding, collaborate with peers, and win exciting prizes in department-wide academic events that celebrate curiosity and ingenuity. A great stage to push your limits and connect with like-minded peers.
