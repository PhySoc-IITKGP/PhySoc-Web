Seminars

Our seminars at PhySoc go beyond textbook physics to what it really means to be a physicist. We regularly invite famous professors and researchers to come and talk about their undergraduate years, the reality of research and give true insights into the life of a physicist. These sessions are meant to inspire, guide, and support you in your own academic journey.” Any questions or want to get involved? Please reach out to Rudraksh! (rudraksh791025@kgpian.iitkgp.ac.in).
