What is ?

Ansatz is the official web magazine of the Physics Society (PhySoc) at the Department of Physics, IIT Kharagpur, with a rich history dating back to foundational departmental archives. Historically, it celebrated the milestones and legacy of our luminaries, including seminal tributes to pioneers like Prof. Sudhansu Datta Majumdar, but it has since evolved to encompass cutting-edge student research, scientific essays, creative physics writing, and colloquium insights.

The magazine is written and curated entirely by students, featuring articles about physics, research, ideas, and topics that writers are passionate about sharing. From foundational mechanics and quantum explorations to creative reflections on scientific concepts, Ansatz provides a vibrant space for students to communicate science in their own voice.

Want to Contribute to Ansatz

Are you a student with a passion for physics, science communication, or just a topic you want to share? Ansatz is written entirely by students, and we welcome contributions from anyone in the IIT Kharagpur physics community.

All articles involve the writers actively in the editing process and are reviewed before publication. This makes contributing a great way to gain experience in writing, revising, and presenting scientific content, giving you valuable practice for producing research articles in the future.

Whether you want to write a popular science article, an academic piece, creative physics non-fiction, or a quirky paradox, we'd love to hear from you!
