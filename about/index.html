Our Mission

To cultivate a vibrant intellectual community where every student can explore physics beyond the classroom — through lectures, research, collaboration, and experimentation.

  • Weekly talks, seminars and reading groups on frontier physics
  • Facilitate student-faculty research connections (UROP)
  • Maintain shared study and resource repositories for all years
  • Represent physics students on academic governance bodies
