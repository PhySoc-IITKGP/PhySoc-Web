About Us

The Physics Society
of IIT Kharagpur

Founded on the belief that curiosity is the cornerstone of discovery, PhySoc has been the heartbeat of physics culture at IIT KGP. We are students, researchers, and dreamers — united by a love for the universe.

Meet the Team Physics Department
🎯

Our Mission

To foster an inclusive, vibrant physics community at IIT Kharagpur that bridges classroom learning with frontier research. We empower students to ask bold questions, build real-time simulations, and collaborate across disciplines.

🚀

Our Vision

To make physics accessible, interactive, and inspiring for every student. From freshman mechanics to advanced general relativity, PhySoc is the launchpad for future physicists, engineers, and scientific leaders.
